YouPod Terms & Privacy

Last updated 12 October 2026

YouPod is a free, click-wheel music player made by Aaron. It plays music from YouTube. This page explains the rules for using it and what happens to your information. It's written in plain words, so it's quick to read.

Terms of use

  • By using YouPod, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, because all the music comes from YouTube.
  • YouPod is free. Nobody is charged to use it or to watch anything in it.
  • You need to be old enough to use YouTube where you live (in most places that's 13).
  • The music and videos belong to their owners. YouPod doesn't download, copy or save any videos. It only shows YouTube's own player.
  • YouPod is a hobby project, provided as it is. It might sometimes break or change.
  • YouPod isn't made by, or connected to, YouTube, Google or Apple.

Privacy policy

YouPod uses YouTube API Services: the YouTube player and YouTube's search. When you use those parts, Google's privacy rules apply too. Read the Google Privacy Policy.

What YouPod saves

YouPod has no accounts, no server and no tracking. Everything it remembers is saved only in your own browser on your device. Aaron can't see any of it.

  • Your settings, like colours, volume and battery level
  • Your YouPod playlists
  • The songs you've played recently (used by For You)
  • Recent search results, kept for 12 hours to save YouTube searches

Song names you've saved are refreshed from YouTube at least every 30 days. If a song can't be refreshed, YouPod removes its old name.

What goes to other companies

  • YouTube / Google: the words you search for, and the videos you play. YouTube's player runs in privacy-enhanced mode, but YouTube may still use cookies or similar technology, and may show ads in its player. YouPod doesn't add any ads of its own.
  • Google Fonts: YouPod's lettering loads from Google, which can see your device's internet address.
  • GitHub: YouPod's website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep basic visit logs.

Deleting your information

  • On the YouPod, go to Settings → Delete My Data. That wipes everything YouPod saved in this browser.
  • Or clear this website's data in your browser's settings.
  • To manage what Google keeps, visit your Google Account.

Questions

You can ask a question on YouPod's GitHub page: github.com/IsgoodTF2/IBPOD. If these terms change, the date at the top changes too.
